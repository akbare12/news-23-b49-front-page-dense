One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asian
markets (Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it's too late,
and the US lead over China falling to a record low — plus 28 more stories from
six regions, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box for what did
*not* happen on 23 November.

Run `python3 main.py` (stdlib only) to copy `entry.png` into
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`; `python3 draw.py` regenerates the image.
